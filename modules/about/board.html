President

Nick Messing

he/him president@sffr.org +
Hometown Lincoln, Nebraska (Go Big Red!)
Member since 2023
Current shoes HOKA Clifton 9
Hobbies Wine tasting and football

A proud running accomplishment

Nick has completed a half marathon in every U.S. state, plus ten more. That is 60 half marathons and counting!

A favorite SFFR memory

The day after attending SFFR for the first time, Nick volunteered at the club's Bay to Breakers water station. Afterward, a group spent the day together in Golden Gate Park, where he met many people who remain close friends today.

Fun fact

Nick has always wanted to be a famous actor and says he would drop everything to move to Hollywood if the right opportunity came along. 🎬

Beyond running

Nick loves exploring the Bay Area through wine tasting. He is also a devoted football fan who roots for the San Francisco 49ers, Green Bay Packers, and Nebraska Cornhuskers.
