From the SFFR bylaws

Board positions

Open a position to review a concise summary of its responsibilities. You may also contact the current Board members to learn more about serving.

⭐

President

+
  • Act as the chief administrative officer and legal head of the FrontRunners.
  • Exercise supervision over the FrontRunners and all its activities and members.
  • Represent and speak on behalf of the FrontRunners to other organizations and to the public.
  • Call meetings.
  • Preside at membership and officer-director meetings.
  • Appoint committee chairpersons.
  • Sign letters or documents necessary to carry out the decisions and intent of the members.
  • Serve as President of the Board of Directors.
🤝

Vice President

+
  • Assume the duties of the President in the case of absence or incapacity, and become President upon the death, resignation, or permanent incapacity of the President.
  • Direct the activities of committees as assigned by the President.
  • Until the establishment of a formal Pride Run committee, assume responsibility for contacting potential major Pride Run sponsors, keeping records, and publishing notes.
  • Serve as Vice President of the Board of Directors.
📝

Secretary

+
  • Take notes of membership meeting proceedings as a basis for preparing minutes.
  • Prepare and certify the correctness of the minutes and enter them in the official record and archive.
  • Read papers, documents, or communications as directed by the President at meetings.
  • Bring the official record of minutes, bylaws, rules, policies, membership list, committee list, and parliamentary authority to each membership meeting.
  • Preserve and upload the records, reports, and official documents of the FrontRunners in digital format, except those assigned to others.
  • Maintain the official record of paid members.
  • Prepare and send required notices of meetings and proposals.
  • Carry on official correspondence except correspondence assigned to other officers or committee chairs.
  • Direct the activities of committees as assigned by the President.
  • Serve as Secretary of the Board of Directors.
💰

Treasurer

+
  • Be responsible for the collection, safekeeping, and expenditure of all FrontRunners funds in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles.
  • Develop a fiscally sound budget with input from officer-directors and committee chairs.
  • Present a brief financial status report at each membership and Board meeting, and publish a brief financial report quarterly.
  • Submit a full report to the membership annually.
  • Direct the activities of committees as assigned by the President.
  • Serve as Treasurer of the Board of Directors.
🏃

Team Captain

+
  • Direct and facilitate the Team Race Circuit, including organizing SFFR teams, keeping rosters, and collecting team results at designated running events.
  • Recruit and retain queer runners of all abilities from the broader San Francisco running community by promoting visibility and team spirit.
  • Create the Team Race Circuit schedule, taking member race and event submissions into consideration.
  • Provide a competitive outlet for members.
  • Serve as Team Captain of the Board of Directors.
  • Currently serve as a non-voting Board member.
🧩

Officer at Large

+
  • Assist all other officers in their duties as needed.
  • Temporarily assume the duties of other officers, with the exception of the President when the Vice President is available, in the case of absence or incapacity.
  • Serve as Officer of the Board of Directors.
💻

Director of Technology

+
  • Maintain and administer the club’s website, email accounts, social media accounts, membership database, communication platforms, and other digital tools.
  • Ensure technology systems are secure, reliable, and accessible to officers and members.
  • Support officers in the use of club technology resources.
  • Evaluate and recommend technology solutions to improve club operations.
  • Oversee and manage the club’s digital membership systems, including online groups, and ensure these platforms are organized and aligned with the club’s membership policies.
