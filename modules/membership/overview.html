Become a Member

Become part of San Francisco’s LGBTQ+ running and walking community! SF FrontRunners welcomes runners, walkers, and everyone in between.

Join a community that runs, walks, races, socializes, volunteers, and supports one another throughout the year.

Membership Levels

🌈 Community Membership

$24/year

Available for students, seniors (65+), active military, and members experiencing financial hardship.

🏃 Standard Membership

$36/year

Our most popular option for members who want to participate fully in SF FrontRunners activities.

⭐ Contributor Membership

$100/year

Provide additional support to help SF FrontRunners continue creating inclusive community experiences.

Membership Benefits

🏃 Weekly Activities

Participate in weekly runs, walks, and FrontRunner events throughout the year.

🌈 Community & Connection

Build friendships with members who share your passion for fitness and community.

🗳️ Have a Voice

Vote, participate in club business, and help shape the future of SF FrontRunners.

🤝 Leadership Opportunities

Serve as an officer, board member, committee chair, or volunteer leader.

🏅 Race Community

Represent SF FrontRunners at races and celebrate achievements together.

📬 Stay Connected

Receive Footweb, club updates, event announcements, and member communications.

Membership Dues Information

New member dues are prorated based on the quarter of the year you join. A new member is anyone who has not been an SF FrontRunners member within the past twelve (12) months.
