From finish line to leaderboard

Four simple steps

Choose a race

Use the official calendar for Race Circuit events, or complete a certified event anywhere for the Global Leaderboard.

Complete the event

Run, walk, or participate virtually when the selected event offers that option.

Submit your result

Complete the Race Completion Form. For a non-circuit race, select “GLOBAL LEADERBOARD” in the event dropdown.

Follow your progress

Check the appropriate leaderboard for your recorded races, points, and season totals.

Race Circuit recognition

Milestones worth celebrating

Recognition and gift cards apply to the Race Circuit Leaderboard. The official rules control eligibility and award details.

6+

Race Circuit Finisher

Earn at least six Race Circuit points during the season.

24

Finisher gift cards

The first 24 Race Circuit Finishers receive a $25 gift card at the End-of-Year Party.

2

Runners of the Year

Two members are recognized for the most points or the most completed races. Each receives a $200 gift card.

How prizes are funded: Some funds SFFR receives from volunteering at race water stations are earmarked for the Race Circuit program.
