Who can participate

The circuit is for active SFFR members

Whether you are chasing a personal best or simply looking for a fun race with friends, there is a place for you.

Eligibility

  • Active SF FrontRunners members who have paid their 2026 dues
  • In-person and organizer-supported virtual participation
  • Official calendar races completed before the End-of-Year Party
  • Qualifying California FrontRunners events of 5K or longer
