Race Circuit Basics

What is the purpose of the SFFR Race Circuit?

It encourages members to support their health and well-being through local and regional running and walking events.

How long does the Race Circuit run?

From February 1 through the day before the December 12, 2026 End-of-Year Party.

Which events count?

Only events on the official 2026 Race Calendar count toward the Race Circuit.

Can walkers participate?

Yes. Many 5K events welcome walkers, and some 10K and virtual events do too.

Points and Reporting

How do I report a completed race?

Complete the Race Completion Form after an eligible event.

How do I become a Race Circuit Finisher?

Earn at least six points. The first 24 finishers receive a $25 gift card at the End-of-Year Party.

Leaderboards and Recognition

What is the difference between the leaderboards?

The Race Circuit Leaderboard tracks official calendar events and local participation. The Global Leaderboard recognizes certified events completed anywhere in the world, including Circuit events.

How do I become Runner of the Year?

Score the most points or complete the most races. Each of the two honorees receives a $200 gift card.

Does the Global Leaderboard offer prizes?

Not currently. It offers recognition, encouragement, and a shared record of member achievements.
