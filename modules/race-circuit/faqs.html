Race Circuit basics

What is the purpose of the SFFR Race Circuit?

The Race Circuit encourages members to support their health and well-being through running and walking while participating in local and regional events throughout the greater Bay Area and California.

Where can I read the official rules?

Read the 2026 Race Circuit Rules. The rules are the final source for point values, eligibility, recognition, and awards.

How long does the Race Circuit run?

The 2026 Race Circuit begins February 1 and ends a day before the club's End-of-Year Party (12th December, 2026).

Which events count toward the Race Circuit?

Only events included on the official 2026 Race Calendar count toward the Race Circuit. The calendar also includes available discount codes and special registration links.

Can walkers participate?

Yes. Many 5K events welcome walkers, and some 10K events do as well. Virtual events can also offer walkers more distance options. Check each race organizer's website for time limits and participation details.

Points and reporting

Why does the Race Circuit use points?

Points provide a simple way to track progress, encourage participation, and add some friendly competition for members who enjoy it.

How do I report a completed race?

After finishing an eligible event, complete the Race Completion Form. Reminders may also be shared in the Races WhatsApp group and the weekly Footweb.

How do I become a Race Circuit Finisher?

Earn at least six points from eligible Race Circuit events. The first 24 finishers receive a $25 gift card at the End-of-Year Party.

Where can I check my progress?

Visit the Race Circuit Leaderboard to see your recorded races and current points.

Leaderboards and recognition

What is the purpose of the leaderboards?

The leaderboards document members' accomplishments, encourage progress, and make it easier for the club to celebrate everyone's effort. Friendly competition is welcome, but community recognition is the larger goal.

What is the difference between the two leaderboards?

The Race Circuit Leaderboard tracks official calendar events and local community participation. The Global Leaderboard recognizes certified running and walking events completed anywhere in the world, including Race Circuit events.

See the full comparison on How the Leaderboards Work.

How do I become Race Circuit Runner of the Year?

Score the most points or complete the most races. Two Race Circuit Runner of the Year recognitions are presented at the End-of-Year Party (12th Dec 2026), each with a $200 gift card prize.

Where does prize funding come from?

Some of the money SFFR receives by volunteering at water stations for race events is earmarked for Race Circuit prizes.

Which races can be submitted to the Global Leaderboard?

Any certified walking or running event held anywhere in the world may be included if it is completed between January 1 and the day before the End-of-Year Party (12th Dec, 2026). When submitting, choose “GLOBAL LEADERBOARD” in the event dropdown.

Does the Global Leaderboard offer prizes?

Not currently. It offers recognition, encouragement, bragging rights, and a shared record of members' achievements. SFFR may explore a prize budget in future years.
