Eligibility

Active SF FrontRunners members who have paid their 2026 dues may participate.

  • Complete an eligible 2026 Race Calendar event.
  • Complete it before the SFFR End-of-Year Party.
  • Qualifying California FrontRunners events of 5K or longer may also earn points.

Awards & Recognition

Race Circuit Finisher: Earn at least six points. Twenty-four finisher awards are available.

Runner of the Year: Recognized for most points and most races completed.
