Member spotlight

Meet Kevin Chan

Kevin Chan

SFFR member since June 2026

Mountain View, California New Balance 1080s Pickleball
Favorite running/walking shoes

New Balance 1080s—and Kevin recommends checking out the women’s colorways, which he thinks are much better.

Biggest running/walking accomplishment

Kevin’s running career is only eight months old, but it has moved fast. After struggling to break 10 minutes for a mile in March, he entered the SFFR Pride Run 10K and placed fourth in his age group in 46:34. He then trained for his first marathon and finished the Humboldt Redwoods Marathon in October in 3:23:24—17th overall, at a 7:45-per-mile pace.

Favorite SFFR memory

On his first attempted Saturday run, Kevin arrived at the boathouse at 9:30 AM after misreading the website and found that everyone had already left. He walked around the lake, waited for runners to return, then ran up the driveway behind them as though he had just finished too. He now knows the run starts at 9:00.

Fun or unexpected fact

Since taking up running, Kevin hardly takes Muni anymore. He has more fun running or walking to places and exploring the city along the way.

Favorite non-running/walking hobby

Pickleball—Kevin usually plays on Saturdays after brunch with the SFFR crew.
