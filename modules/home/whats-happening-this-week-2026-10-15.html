Member spotlight

Meet Will Hsieh

Will Hsieh

SFFR member since August 2021

Taichung & Santo Domingo On Cloudrunner 3 Piano
Favorite running/walking shoes

“I’m not a shoe connoisseur, but I really like my On Cloudrunner 3. It just feels so comfortable and well-supported. I got those after a quick gait analysis at Sports Basement.”

Biggest running/walking accomplishment

Since joining SFFR in 2021, Will ran his fastest 5K at the Valencia Gay Games, despite temperatures above 80°F. Maybe he is a glutton for punishment, or maybe he is simply not a morning runner.

Favorite SFFR memory

It is impossible to choose from memories like the 50th Anniversary dinner, Beach Chalet holiday party, Pride soirées, an overnight kayaking trip to Angel Island, and countless Saturday brunches. The constant is getting to know fellow FrontRunners outside of running. Will values how diverse, talented, fun, and genuinely kind the community is.

Fun or unexpected fact

Before the pandemic, Will swam with Tsunami and brunched with his swim friends on Saturday mornings while Ted ran and brunched with FrontRunners. When SFFR restarted before gyms and pools reopened, FOMO brought Will along. He still swims with Tsunami twice a week, but Saturday mornings are now reserved for SFFR because they are more social and fun.

Favorite non-running/walking hobby

Playing the piano, one of the few things that can distract him from scrolling on his phone.
