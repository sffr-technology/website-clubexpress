Member spotlight

Meet Chase Skelton

Chase Skelton

SFFR member since July 2023

Wichita, Kansas On Cloudrunner Home chef
Favorite running/walking shoes

On Cloudrunner: so comfortable and stylish.

Biggest running/walking accomplishment

Completing his first 50K at the 2015 Full Moon 50K on logging roads through Arkansas’s Ouachita National Forest. The July race began at 9:00 PM to beat the heat, though temperatures still approached 100°F with intense humidity. Chase jumped over copperheads, hopped over tarantulas, startled a black bear, and crossed the finish line around 2:00 AM. He had so much fun that he returned the following year.

Favorite SFFR memory

Racing the N Judah from Carl and Cole to Ocean Beach with a group of SFFR friends. Their friends and partners rode the train and heckled the runners from the windows at every stop. Chase narrowly missed beating the train by about 30 seconds, but still loved the rush of racing against the machine.

Fun or unexpected fact

Chase spent the past year reclaiming Luxembourg citizenship through his great-great-great-grandfather Pierre. He is now a proud dual citizen of a country he has not yet visited and whose population is smaller than San Francisco’s.

Favorite non-running/walking hobby

Cooking. Whether it’s a weeknight dinner for his partner Alex, serving as chef de cuisine for a weekend with friends, or preparing a holiday feast, Chase is happiest and most creative in the kitchen.
