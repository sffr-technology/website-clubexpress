On August 30, we hosted a Drag Brunch Fundraiser to raise additional funds for our Pride Run beneficiary and show our appreciation to our sponsors. We are excited to announce that we raised $43,500 for the Rafiki Coalition!

Thank you to everyone who participated in or volunteered for the Pride Run, as well as everyone who attended the drag brunch. SFFR members never cease to amaze with their generosity and hard work. Great job to everyone who helped make it happen!

In memoriam

Passing of Former SFFR Member Alan Weaver

We are saddened to share the passing of Alan Weaver, an active SFFR member from the late 1970s through much of the 1980s. A lifelong lover of hiking and wildflowers, Alan co-founded the Gay/Lesbian Sierrans, the Sierra Club’s first LGBTQ section, and served the community through several civic and labor organizations.

Member spotlight

Meet Matthew Kaea

Matthew Kaea

SFFR member since 2024

Napa, Idaho 2:52 marathon World Marathon Majors finisher
Biggest running/walking accomplishment

Matthew ran a personal-best 2:52 marathon at the 2019 Seoul Marathon, finishing inside the 1988 Olympic Stadium after catching the first wave’s three-hour pace group. He has also completed all of the World Marathon Majors, most in under three hours, along with marathons in Paris, Vancouver, and Barcelona.

Favorite SFFR memory

Volunteering at the 2026 San Francisco Marathon water station, from preparing cups to cheering on the runners. Matthew also finally convinced a buddy to join SFFR because of the book club.

Fun or unexpected fact

Matthew has completed the Escape from Alcatraz Triathlon seven years in a row and now has guaranteed entry. He has also completed numerous Olympic triathlons, half-Ironman races, and one full Ironman.

Favorite running/walking shoes

Nike is Matthew’s go-to brand. He wears the Alphafly 3 for Monday and Thursday speed and hill workouts, and the Winflo 12 for Saturday long runs.

Favorite non-running/walking hobby

Matthew recently joined the SFFR book club and often reads three or four books at once. He also enjoys gardening, especially growing colorful flowers that attract butterflies and hummingbirds.
