Member spotlight

Meet Tim Allen

Tim Allen

SFFR member since 1991

East Lansing, Michigan Keen Targhee hiking shoes Samuel Hubbard Free oxfords
Favorite running and walking shoes

Tim wears Keen Targhee hiking shoes and Samuel Hubbard Free oxfords with leather uppers and Vibram soles. He uses SOLE Active Medium insoles in both.

Proudest accomplishment

For decades, Tim ran 20–25 miles a week. After having his knees replaced, he switched to walking and now covers 35–40 miles a week.

Unexpected fact

He builds acoustic guitars, an activity called “luthierie,” and serves as President of the Northern California Association of Luthiers.

Favorite non-running and walking hobby

Playing the guitar and piano.

Favorite SFFR memory

Tim joined SFFR partly hoping to find a boyfriend. After deciding to simply enjoy the running club, he met an attractive, interesting, lovable guy one Saturday morning. This October, they’ll celebrate their 34th anniversary.
