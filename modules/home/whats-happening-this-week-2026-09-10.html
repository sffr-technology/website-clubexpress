Member spotlight

Meet Devesh Khatu

Devesh Khatu

SFFR member since 2003

Mumbai and Pune, India ASICS Gel-Kayano Crosswords
Favorite running/walking shoes

“I pronate, which limits my choices in shoes. I wear a stability shoe; for the last 10 or so years, the ASICS Gel-Kayano has served me well.”

Biggest running/walking accomplishment

Qualifying for the Boston Marathon with a marathon personal record of 3:19 in Chicago.

Favorite SFFR memory

Making friends through the club. At the 2007 SFFR annual banquet, Gary Pfizer approached Devesh after he received an award. They got together shortly afterward and have now been best friends for nearly 20 years.

Fun or unexpected fact

“I wasn’t naturally a runner. I was not at all athletic growing up, focusing more on studies. I only seriously took up running after testing positive for HIV in 2003, by joining SFFR.”

Favorite non-running/walking hobby

Currently, solving and setting crosswords. Devesh also loves travel, food, photography, blogging, movies, museums, and giving back to the community through volunteering and fundraising.
