Member spotlight

Meet Ted Wyder

Ted Wyder

SFFR member since 2012

Milwaukee, Wisconsin Mizuno Wave Rider Cellist
Biggest running/walking accomplishment

Ted is most proud of running his first marathon in Los Angeles in 2012. He initially began running for exercise and never imagined completing a marathon. After joining LA Frontrunners and eventually attending their Sunday long runs, their encouragement and support helped him reach the full 26.2 miles.

Favorite SFFR memory

Attending the 2023 Gay Games in Guadalajara, where Ted competed in the 5K, 10K, and half marathon alongside other SFFR members. A group rented a house, made dinners, went sightseeing, and attended nightly socials with other FrontRunners. He recommends that everyone attend a Gay Games at least once.

Fun or unexpected fact

Ted’s graduate degree is in astronomy. He worked as an astronomer in Los Angeles before moving to the Bay Area and changing careers to tech.

Favorite non-running/walking hobby

Playing the cello with the Bay Area Rainbow Symphony.
