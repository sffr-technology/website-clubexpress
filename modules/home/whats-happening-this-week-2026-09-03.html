ASICS Gel-Cumulus 27.

Biggest running/walking accomplishment

(Cue nostalgic soundtrack.) Clayton ran his first marathon in San Francisco in 1996 after several rough years working science jobs with an undergraduate biology degree. He had been running for five years and prepared with training runs whose distances he estimated from paper maps. SFFR members encouraged him along the way. He finished in under three hours, only later realizing that was an important benchmark when club members called to congratulate him. The next day, a trophy for third place in his age division arrived on his doorstep. He remembers that nothing could upset him for the rest of that week.

Favorite SFFR memory

“I love that so many SFFR people also come to the BARS concerts and other arts activities. I love our expanded community.”

Fun or unexpected fact

“I take harpsichord lessons and conduct myself with the restraint and decorum of a virtuous 18th century princess.”
