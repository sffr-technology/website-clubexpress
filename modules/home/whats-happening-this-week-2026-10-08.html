Thank you to everyone who helped at the Castro Street Fair this past Sunday! It was a beautiful day to introduce SFFR to the community and recruit new members. Our new branded swag was a hit, and we may have extras to share at the year end party.

We’re excited to announce that Billy Tang has been appointed the 2027 Pride Run Race Director! Meet him in the Member Spotlight below. We look forward to working alongside him on another outstanding Pride Run.

Member spotlight

Meet Billy Tang

Billy Tang

2027 Pride Run Race Director · SFFR member since 2023

Rowland Heights, CAMiddle school principalChorus singer
Biggest running/walking accomplishment

Completing the Tough Ruck in honor of the fallen first responders of the Boston Marathon bombing. Billy carried a 50 lb rucksack for all 26.2 miles, alongside his partner at the time, who was in the military.

Favorite SFFR memory

Doing the overnight Ragnar relay at Lake Tahoe. The group had plenty of downtime between legs to get to know each other and cheer each other on.

Fun or unexpected fact

Billy is a middle school principal.

Favorite running/walking shoes

Brooks Adrenaline GTS.

Favorite non-running/walking hobby

Singing with the San Francisco Gay Men’s Chorus.
