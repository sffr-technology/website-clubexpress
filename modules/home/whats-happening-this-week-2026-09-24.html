Member spotlight

Meet Christof Wittig

Christof Wittig

SFFR member since 2009

Munich, GermanyJohn Muir Trail finisherUltra trail runner
Biggest running/walking accomplishment

Christof recently completed a fully supported run of the John Muir Trail, covering more than 230 miles in under six days. His endurance journey has taken him from city marathons to triathlons, including a full Ironman, and all three UTMB finals. He now enjoys self-organized adventures that combine travel, community, and nature. Read about his John Muir Trail project →

Favorite SFFR memory

Representing San Francisco alongside a large group of visiting FrontRunners at the Sydney Marathon in August, and being part of a strong SFFR contingent at the Valencia Gay Games, where he competed in the Olympic triathlon and several runs.

Fun or unexpected fact

Christof never ran more than 5K until age 32. Before then, he was not especially interested in sports.

Favorite running/walking shoes

Hoka Mafate 5 for trails and Adidas Evo 1 for road races.

Favorite non-running/walking hobby

Christof enjoys connecting people, both through his work at Hornet and elsewhere. He loves helping people make friends or find partners.
