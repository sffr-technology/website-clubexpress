Meet James Kraemer

James Kraemer

SFFR member since May 2026

Palo Alto, CASaucony Endorphin Speed 5Restaurants & bars
Favorite running/walking shoes

Saucony Endorphin Speed 5.

Biggest running/walking accomplishment

Completing his first marathon in Providence, Rhode Island, in 2017. James started running in 2015 and never imagined running farther than a 10K. Training for and finishing a marathon gave him an amazing sense of accomplishment and fulfillment—and confidence in what he could achieve with preparation.

Favorite SFFR memory

After 12 years in Boston, James moved back to San Francisco in May. SFFR’s welcoming community has made his return amazing and filled with new friends.

Fun or unexpected fact

James studied Russian for two years in college and studied abroad in St. Petersburg.

Favorite non-running/walking hobby

Trying new restaurants and bars.
