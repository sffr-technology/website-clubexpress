Member spotlight

Meet Michael Kennedy

Michael Kennedy

SFFR member since November 2025

Santa Rosa, California Nike ZoomX Pegasus Concert fan
Favorite running/walking shoes

Michael often chooses his running shoes based on color and is fortunate that his feet are comfortable in most brands. He is currently enjoying the Nike ZoomX Pegasus in neon pink.

Biggest running/walking accomplishment

Completing the 2024 Cascade Express Marathon in Washington in under three hours. Michael was on a fire assignment and did not know until the day before whether he could participate. He rented a car, drove to the race, and slept in the parking lot. Simply being happy to make it put him in the right mindset, and the result sparked his goal of one day qualifying for the Boston Marathon.

Favorite SFFR memory

His very first day. Within moments of arriving at Blue Heron Lake, then-president Mike Terry welcomed him and gave him a ribbon, while Ricky Gonzales and Peter Rigano ran the full five-mile route with him. Having visited many run clubs, Michael values how warm, welcoming, inclusive, and community-minded SFFR was from the very beginning.

Fun or unexpected fact

Michael is a triplet, with a brother and sister the same age. He also has two more sisters, one older and one younger, making him one of five children.

Favorite non-running/walking hobby

Seeing live music and attending concerts.
