Recurring opportunities

Make a regular moment possible

These roles create reliable touchpoints for members. Open a role to see what is involved.

👟 Shoe Donation Coordinator

Organize a twice-yearly collection of gently used, wearable shoes from club members. Gather the donations and deliver them to local thrift stores or community organizations for reuse.

Monthly Supper Club Host

Choose a restaurant, set the details, welcome attendees, and help members connect around the table.

Monthly Social Host

Plan an approachable social activity that gives new and longtime members another way to spend time together.

Monthly Movie Night Host

Select a film and coordinate a casual movie outing or gathering for members.

Digital Storyteller & Community Engagement Lead

Help share member stories, events, milestones, and community moments through SFFR’s digital channels.

Form 1023 Project Team

Three work groups helped prepare SFFR's federal tax-exemption application.

Governance & Bylaws: Reviewed governing documents and helped ensure the application accurately reflected how SFFR is organized and operated.

Programs & Operations: Described the club's activities, programs, community impact, and day-to-day operations.

Finance & Accounting: Helped assemble financial information and projections with historical context and support.

Every contribution helps SF FrontRunners stay welcoming, active, and connected.
