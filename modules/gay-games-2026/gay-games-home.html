A shared legacy

FrontRunners and the Gay Games grew up together

Our connection reaches back to the beginning. SF FrontRunners was already building LGBTQ+ community through running when the first Gay Games brought athletes from around the world to San Francisco.

1974

SF FrontRunners begins

Eight years before the first Gay Games, the country’s first FrontRunners club began bringing LGBTQ+ runners together in San Francisco.

1982

The first Gay Games

Dr. Tom Waddell’s vision came to life in San Francisco. The Games gave LGBTQ+ athletes and FrontRunners greater visibility and helped inspire new clubs.

1986

SFFR helps make it happen

For Gay Games II, SFFR marathoners Mary Ann Powers and Bob Puerzer headed the Marathon Committee. FrontRunners from around the world also held their first international gathering.

2026

The tradition reaches Valencia

SFFR members carried that legacy to Spain as athletes, supporters, volunteers, travelers, and part of a worldwide FrontRunner community.

As the Gay Games traveled from San Francisco to cities around the world, FrontRunner clubs grew into an international network connected by sport, friendship, and visibility. Valencia is the newest chapter in a relationship spanning more than four decades. Explore the Gay Games archive and International Front Runners history.
