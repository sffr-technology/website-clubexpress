2026 beneficiary

Rafiki Coalition for Health and Wellness

Rafiki Coalition works to eliminate health inequities in San Francisco's Black, LGBTQ+, and marginalized communities through education, advocacy, and culturally affirming health and wellness services.

Our shared commitment to wellness, fitness, inclusion, and pride helps make health and well-being more accessible throughout the year.
