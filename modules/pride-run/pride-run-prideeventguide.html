Getting here

Please use public transportation, bike, walk, or carpool whenever possible.

Transit and parking

  • N Judah to Judah Street and 34th Avenue
  • 29 Muni to Lincoln Way and 33rd Avenue
  • If driving, park on JFK Drive near the Park Stables and walk via the Polo Field's upper track
No parking near the start

Parking on Middle Drive is unavailable. Parking is prohibited on MLK Drive between Elk Glen Meadow and Chain of Lakes Drive; illegally parked vehicles may be cited and towed.

Participate anywhere

Virtual event and kids' dash

Virtual 5K and 10K

  • Complete either distance anywhere from June 13–28
  • Receive a shirt at bib pickup or by mail within 30 days after the event
  • Virtual events do not offer top-finisher medals
  • Participants may register for the shirt even if they do not complete a run

Kids' dash and awards

The youth dash is 50 meters. Young runners who want a shirt and bib register through the standard process; otherwise, a parent or guardian completes a waiver at registration.

Medals recognize the top three overall finishers in men's, women's, and nonbinary divisions, plus gender-identity divisions in age groups from under 20 through 90+.
