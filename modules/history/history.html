At a glance

Milestones along the route

  1. 1973Lavender U. lists an introduction to jogging class.
  2. 1974The first run begins near the south windmill in Golden Gate Park.
  3. 1978The group reorganizes with members, bylaws and elected officers.
  4. 1980The first Gay Run draws approximately 400 participants.
  5. 1998A club water fountain is installed in Golden Gate Park.
  6. 1999The Little Black Dress Run tradition begins.
  7. 2020A commemorative bronze plaque is installed at Stow Lake.
  8. TodayThe club continues to run, walk, volunteer and build community.
