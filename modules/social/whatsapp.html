There is even more to explore

The groups featured here are only a selection, not the full list. Once you join the SFFR WhatsApp Community, you can browse even more groups and find the conversations that fit your interests.

Members are also welcome to suggest new groups that could help people connect, participate, and engage with the SFFR community.

A few group guidelines

Help us keep the SFFR WhatsApp community welcoming, useful, and inclusive for everyone.

Read the full Community Guidelines →
  • Be respectful and kind to fellow members.
  • Keep conversations relevant to each group's purpose.
  • Avoid excessive posting or repeating the same message across multiple groups.
  • Use Random / Off Topic when a conversation does not fit elsewhere.
