Why we have these guidelines

SF FrontRunners is built on connection, inclusion, and community. With hundreds of members participating in different conversations, these guidelines help everyone feel welcome, respected, and comfortable engaging in our community spaces.

They are not meant to limit personal expression. They help keep official SFFR spaces focused on our shared mission: bringing people together through running, friendship, and community.

🌈 Inclusive

All identities, paces, and experiences belong.

🤝 Respectful

Kindness and consideration guide every exchange.

❤️ Community First

Our shared spaces should support and uplift members.

🤝

Be Respectful

  • Treat everyone with kindness and respect.
  • Use inclusive language and honor people's pronouns.
  • No harassment, discrimination, or hate speech of any kind.
💬

Keep It Relevant

  • Use each chat for its intended purpose.
  • Avoid excessive messaging, spam, or repeating the same message across groups.
  • Be mindful of message volume, especially in large groups.
  • Use Random / Off Topic for conversations that do not fit elsewhere.
🗳️

Politics

SFFR is a social and athletic organization, not a political one. We encourage civic engagement and recognize that many political issues are personally important to our members.

As a tax-exempt nonprofit organization, SFFR must remain nonpartisan and cannot support or oppose candidates for public office. Members may participate in political activities in their personal capacities, but campaign-related advocacy or election content should not be shared in official SFFR community spaces in a way that could be interpreted as representing the organization.

  • Posts related to political candidates, campaigns, or elections are not permitted in official SFFR chats.
  • Please keep discussions focused on running, community, social events, and member connection.
  • Moderators may remove political content to help maintain an inclusive and welcoming environment.
🔒

Privacy Matters

  • Do not share another person's contact details, personal information, or screenshots without consent.
  • Respect the confidentiality of conversations.
  • Do not share the protected community invitation outside SFFR.
🏃

Running & Events

  • Share runs, races, and meetups in their designated chats.
  • Encourage and support runners and walkers of every level.
  • Make it clear when an activity is member-organized rather than an official SFFR event.
🚫

What Is Not Allowed

  • Harassment, bullying, discrimination, or exclusionary behavior.
  • Unsolicited promotions or unrelated advertising.
  • Explicit content.
  • Sharing private member information without permission.
⚠️

Moderation

Admins may step in when these guidelines are not followed. Depending on the situation, content may be removed and a member may receive a reminder or warning. Repeated or serious violations may result in removal from the community.

If something makes you uncomfortable or appears to violate these guidelines, contact the Board privately rather than escalating the issue in the group.

Community First

Let’s keep this space safe, fun, useful, and inclusive for everyone.
