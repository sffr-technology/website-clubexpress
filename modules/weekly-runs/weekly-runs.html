Weekly Runs & Walks

Find Your Pace. Find Your Community.

Anyone is welcome to join our weekly runs and walks! Simply show up at any of our weekly meeting points!

Whether you’re training for a marathon, getting back into running, or simply looking for a fun way to meet people, you’ll find a welcoming community at SF FrontRunners.

After attending a few runs, we encourage you to support the club by becoming a member.

What To Expect

👋 Meet the Group

Before the run/walk begins, everyone introduces themselves and the Run Coordinator shares announcements about upcoming club events and activities.

🏃 Run or Walk Your Way

We’ll review suggested routes before heading out together. Choose a pace and distance that feels comfortable.

🍻 Meet Back & Connect

About 50 minutes later, we’ll return to the starting point to wrap up together. Many members stick around afterward to socialize, grab food, and continue the conversation.
