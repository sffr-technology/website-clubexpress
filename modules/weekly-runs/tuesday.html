Weekly Run & Walk

Tuesday Evening Run

Join San Francisco FrontRunners for a welcoming Tuesday evening run or walk along the Embarcadero. Choose your distance, enjoy views of the bay, and stay for dinner with the group afterward.

About the Run

A group of 15 to 30 runners and walkers meets outside the Ferry Building underneath the clock tower. Choose a 3 mile, 4 mile, or 5 mile route, then join the group for dinner at Gott's afterward.

No bag drop is available. Free street parking is often available near the Bay Club Gateway.

Choose Your Route

All routes begin and end at the Ferry Building. The north and south routes are out and back, so you can turn around whenever you reach your preferred distance.

5 Mile North

1 to 4 mile variants

Our most common route follows the Embarcadero north. It is flat except for the Fort Mason hill and offers scenic views of the Golden Gate Bridge, Alcatraz, and the bay. We regroup at the top of the hill for a brief photo before returning to the Ferry Building.

View on Strava

5 Mile South

1 to 4 mile variants

This route follows the Embarcadero south and is flat as a pancake, with scenic views of the marina, Oracle Park, and Chase Center. Turn around at your desired distance before returning to the Ferry Building.

View on Strava

The Hill Run

Daylight saving time only

Our world-famous hill route is offered when evening daylight allows. It adds climbing and city views for runners looking for a more challenging Tuesday option.

View on Strava

Everyone Is Welcome

All ages and abilities are welcome. We are a club of all faces and all paces, and we are proud to include both runners and walkers. Whether you are training for Boston or coming out for an evening walk, you will be in good company.

Run Information

  • The group meets and runs rain or shine.
  • Choose the distance and pace that feel right for you.
  • The north and south routes can easily be shortened.
  • Please obey traffic signals and stay alert at crossings.
  • There is no bag drop available at the Ferry Building.
