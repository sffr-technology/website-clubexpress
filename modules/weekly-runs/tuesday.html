Weekly Run & Walk

Tuesday Evening Run

Join San Francisco FrontRunners for a welcoming Tuesday evening run or walk along the Embarcadero. Choose your distance, enjoy views of the bay, and stay for dinner afterward.

About the Run

  • When: Tuesday at 6:30 PM, rain or shine
  • Meet: SF Ferry Building, under the clock tower
  • Distances: 3, 4, or 5 mile run or walk options
  • Afterward: Dinner at Gott’s

Choose Your Route

5 Mile North

Flat except for the Fort Mason hill, with views of the Golden Gate Bridge, Alcatraz, and the bay. One-to-four-mile variants are easy on this out-and-back route.

5 Mile South

A flat out-and-back route with views of the marina, Oracle Park, and Chase Center.

The Hill Run

Offered during daylight saving time, adding climbing and city views.

Everyone Is Welcome

All ages and abilities are welcome. We are a club of all faces and all paces, and proudly include runners and walkers.

  • Choose the distance and pace that feel right for you.
  • Please obey traffic signals and stay alert at crossings.
  • There is no bag drop at the Ferry Building.
