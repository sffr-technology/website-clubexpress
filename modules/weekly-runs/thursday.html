Weekly Run

Thursday Evening Run

Join San Francisco FrontRunners for a social Thursday evening run from the Eureka Valley library patio through Duboce Park and the Panhandle. Choose a 4 or 5 mile route and stay for dinner with the group afterward.

New meetup location

Moving to the library patio September 10

Beginning Thursday, September 10, meet on the back patio of the Eureka Valley/Harvey Milk Memorial Branch Library, 1 Jose Sarria Ct.

Enter through the main entrance, walk past the main desk to the left, and continue straight back to the patio. Restrooms are available before the run or walk.

About the Run

A group of 50 to 60 runners and walkers meets on the back patio of the Eureka Valley/Harvey Milk Memorial Branch Library. Choose the 4 or 5 mile route, regroup for a photo at Fell and Stanyan, and join the group for dinner afterward.

Choose Your Route

Both routes pass Duboce Park and follow the Panhandle to Fell and Stanyan, where the group stops for a photo. Turn back after the photo for 4 miles or continue to JFK Drive and 8th Avenue for 5 miles.

Starting September 10: The route maps show the established Panhandle courses. Run hosts will guide the group between the new library patio meetup point and the routes.

Panhandle Run

4 miles

Follow the group through Duboce Park and along the Panhandle to Fell and Stanyan. After the group photo, turn back through the Panhandle and return to the library patio.

Extended Panhandle Run

5 miles

Continue beyond Fell and Stanyan to JFK Drive and 8th Avenue before turning back. This option adds roughly one mile to the standard Panhandle route.

Everyone Is Welcome

All ages and abilities are welcome. We are a club of all faces and all paces. Whether you are training for Boston, building toward a first race, or simply joining us for a social run, you will be in good company.

Run Information

  • The group meets and runs rain or shine.
  • Both distances follow the same route until Fell and Stanyan.
  • The route is out and back, so you may turn around earlier if needed.
  • Please obey traffic signals and stay alert at road crossings.
  • There is no bag drop available at the meeting point.
  • Enter through the library's main entrance and continue past the main desk to the back patio.
  • Restrooms are available inside the library before the run or walk.
  • Please use street parking and do not park in the library parking lot.
  • Fitness SF members may continue using its lockers and showers before or after the run.

Dinner Afterwards

After the run, the group usually heads to Hi Tops nearby. Come for the run, the food, or both.
